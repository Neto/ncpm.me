Questions

What does “lifetime upgrades” mean?

You pay $5 once. Every new version of the app you bought is free, for as long as we make the app.

Are there in-app purchases or a subscription?

No. The $5 unlocks the whole app. There is nothing more to buy and nothing renews.

Does it work on my Mac?

Both apps need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). Spin needs macOS 26 or later. Grabb needs macOS 15 or later.

Do I need to sign in to Instagram to use Grabb?

Only for content that needs a login, such as Stories. Grabb reads the cookies of a browser you choose. You never type a password in Grabb.
