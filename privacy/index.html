Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Short version: our apps work on your Mac. We have no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. We only get your data when you buy an app or write to us.

Who we are

Spinoff Software (“we”) makes the Mac apps Spin and Grabb and runs this website. For any privacy question, write to support@ncpm.me.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you open a page and can keep it in its logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Spin

Spin works only on your Mac. It keeps your Spins, the folders in them and their positions in the app's settings on your Mac. Spin does not send your files, folder names or settings to us or to anyone else.

Grabb

  • When you paste a link, Grabb connects to that website to get and download the video. That website receives your IP address, as it does when you open it in a browser.
  • Some websites only show a video when you are signed in. For these, you can choose a browser in Grabb's settings. Grabb then reads that browser's cookies on your Mac and sends them only to the website of the link. Grabb never sends cookies to us.
  • Your downloads and your download history stay on your Mac.
  • Grabb can record a diagnostic log. This is off by default. The log stays on your Mac, and we only see it if you choose to send it to us.
  • Grabb includes yt-dlp, FFmpeg and Deno. These tools run on your Mac and connect only to the website of the link you paste.

Updates

Once a day, Spin and Grabb check for a new version. They download a small update file from GitHub. This request includes your IP address and the name and version of the app. The apps do not send information about your Mac or about how you use them.

Purchases

A payment provider sells the apps for us and acts as the merchant of record. It collects your name, email address, country and payment details, and processes them under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address, country and the product you bought, so we can deliver the app and help you. We never see your card number.

Email

When you write to us, we keep your message and your email address so we can answer you.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising. We do not track you across apps or websites.

How long we keep data

We keep purchase records for as long as tax law requires. We keep emails for as long as we need them to help you, and we delete them when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal data we have about you, and you can object to how we use it. Write to support@ncpm.me. We answer within 15 days. If you live in Brazil, you can also complain to the ANPD. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our apps and this website are not made for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
